# A home for Tales of Souls - the website

Alongside the game we started working on this website. It will become the central place for news, the wiki, the item database, ladders and community features like build lists and recordings.

![website](../img/2024/2024_07_14/website.png)

Many pages are still drafts and use sample data, but the structure is in place. Over time the pages will be connected to the live game data, so you can look up your characters, your guild and your progress directly on the website.
