# Code of Conduct

Tales of Souls should be a welcoming place for everyone. By playing the game or taking part in our community (Discord, forums, streams, feedback board) you agree to follow these rules.

## Be respectful

* No harassment, hate speech, discrimination or threats
* No sexual content involving minors - ever
* Respect the privacy of others; don't share personal information

## Play fair

* No cheating, hacking, botting or use of unauthorized third party tools
* Report bugs and exploits instead of abusing them
* No real money trading of accounts, items or currency

## Keep it clean

* No spam, advertising or scams
* No impersonation of staff or other players
* Use appropriate names for characters, guilds and pets

## Enforcement

Violations can lead to warnings, temporary suspensions or permanent bans depending on their severity. If you see someone breaking these rules, please report them in game or via the [contact form](/contact).
